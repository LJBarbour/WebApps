BET Theory & Guide
The BET Equation

The linearised BET equation is:

p/p₀ / [n(1 − p/p₀)] = 1/(nₘC) + (C−1)/(nₘC) · p/p₀

A plot of the left side (y) vs p/p₀ (x) gives a straight line with slope s = (C−1)/(nₘC) and intercept i = 1/(nₘC), so:

  • nₘ = 1/(s + i) — monolayer capacity
  • C = s/i + 1 — BET C-constant
Rouquerol Consistency Criteria

The valid BET range must satisfy all three criteria:

  1. n(1 − p/p₀) must increase monotonically with p/p₀
  2. The C-constant must be positive
  3. The calculated nₘ must fall within the selected p/p₀ range

The auto-selection finds the longest contiguous window of ≥3 points satisfying criterion 1 and verifies criteria 2 & 3. The Rouquerol plot shows n(1−p/p₀) vs p/p₀ — the selected region is shaded; you can drag its boundary handles to adjust the range manually.

Surface Area Calculation

SBET = nₘ · NA · σ / msample

where nₘ is the monolayer capacity (mol/g), NA is Avogadro's number, σ is the molecular cross-sectional area, and msample is the sample mass.

CSV Format

The file must have two columns: pressure (or p/p₀) and uptake. Comma, tab, or semicolon delimiters are all accepted. Any line that cannot be parsed as two numbers is treated as a metadata or header line.

Recognised pressure units (first column):

p/p₀ or relative (default)  ·  Pa  ·  kPa  ·  MPa  ·  bar  ·  mbar  ·  atm  ·  mmHg / Torr  ·  psi
Absolute pressures are divided by the sorptive's p₀ to give p/p₀.
