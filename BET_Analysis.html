BET Theory & Guide

The BET Equation

The linearised BET equation is:

p/p₀ / [n(1 − p/p₀)] = 1/(nₘC) + (C−1)/(nₘC) · p/p₀

A plot of the left side (y) vs p/p₀ (x) gives a straight line with slope s = (C−1)/(nₘC) and intercept i = 1/(nₘC), so:

  • nₘ = 1/(s + i) — monolayer capacity
  • C = s/i + 1 — BET C-constant

Rouquerol Consistency Criteria

The valid BET range must satisfy:

  1. n(1 − p/p₀) must increase monotonically with p/p₀
  2. The C-constant must be positive
  3. The calculated nₘ must fall within the selected p/p₀ range

The auto-selection finds the longest contiguous window of ≥3 points that satisfies criterion 1, and checks criteria 2 & 3.

Surface Area Calculation

SBET = nₘ · Nₐ · σ / Msample

where nₘ is in mol, Nₐ is Avogadro's number, σ is the molecular cross-sectional area, and Msample is sample mass.

CSV Format

The CSV must have two numeric columns: p/p₀ (0–1) and uptake. Column headers and blank lines are ignored. If the first column exceeds 1, values are assumed to be absolute pressures and are divided by p₀.

You can optionally embed metadata in the header lines (before the data) so the app sets mass, sorptive, and units automatically. Any line that cannot be parsed as two numbers is treated as a header. Recognised formats:

mass = 0.2345
sorptive = N2_77
uptake_unit = mmol/g
relative_pressure, uptake_mmol_g
0.01000, 0.04521
0.02000, 0.05103
...

Key–value pairs use = or : as separator. Keys are case-insensitive. Recognised sorptive names: N2, N2_77, N2_87, Ar, Ar_77, Ar_87, CO2, CO2_273, CO2_298, Kr, H2O, water. Recognised unit strings: cm3/g, cm3, mmol/g, mmol, mg/g, mg. Any fields not found in the file are shown as "assumed" in the banner and default to N₂ at 77 K, mass = 0.1 g, units = mmol (absolute).
