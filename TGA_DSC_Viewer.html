TGA / DSC Viewer

User Guide

Displays thermogravimetric (TGA) and differential scanning calorimetry (DSC) data on a shared temperature axis and turns them into publication-ready figures with quantitative onset, mass-loss and enthalpy analysis. Everything runs locally in the browser; no data leaves this page.

Loading data

  • Drop one or more TGA/DSC files anywhere on the app — the file content is inspected and each one is routed to the correct channel automatically.
  • Encodings: ASCII and UTF-16 LE (TA Instruments) are auto-detected.
  • TA legacy (Universal Analysis): header scanned to StartOfData; a Size line converts mg to %; an Instrument line distinguishes TGA from DSC; the terminal -1 row is discarded.
  • TRIOS TGA: tab-delimited Time / Temperature / Weight / Weight; weight-% column used; initial mass back-calculated.
  • TRIOS DSC: Heat Flow (Normalized) in W/g multiplied by the Sample Mass header to give mW.
  • If the file carries no mass, type it into Sample mass (mg) — this enables enthalpy results and, for legacy mg-based TGA data, conversion to %.

Heating and cooling legs

Multi-ramp DSC runs (heat–cool cycles) are segmented automatically with a sliding-window direction algorithm robust to noise. Each leg is labelled and coloured; individual legs can be hidden with the checkboxes under the DSC zone, and Colour DSC by leg switches between per-leg colours and a single colour.

Analyses (several can coexist)

  • DSC onset: click Pick, then click one point on each side of a peak. Baseline, leading-edge tangent, To, Tp, area and ΔH are computed. The baseline joins the picks; the leading-edge tangent is fitted at the steepest smoothed slope; To is their intersection, Tp the maximum deviation. The peak area is integrated against time (trapezoids, mW·min → mJ) and divided by sample mass for ΔH in J g−1.
  • TGA onset: click Pick, then click the flat plateau before the step, then the steep falling region. Baseline and inflection tangent (steepest point, via DTG when active) intersect at To.
  • Mass loss: click Pick, then click two temperatures on the TGA curve. Each pick adds a bracket annotation showing Δm; several can coexist — add as many steps as the decomposition has.
  • Every annotation label is draggable on the plot. Each result is listed in the panel with a remove button.
  • Annotation font and size are set in the Annotation menu; each annotation's colour always matches the curve (or DSC leg) it labels.

Display conventions

  • T unit: axis in °C or K (data are stored in °C; conversion is display-only).
  • Invert DSC axis and the exo arrow annotation support both exo-up and exo-down conventions.
  • Tick labels share a common decimal count across each axis (scientific-figure convention).
  • Each channel's line colour is set on its TGA/DSC card in the left sidebar, not in the right-hand panel.

Plot interaction

  • drag to zoom the temperature range; right-drag to pan; double-click or Reset-zoom restores the full range.
  • A floating tooltip reports T plus the nearest TGA, DTG and DSC values.
  • The legend, and any onset/peak annotation label, can be dragged directly on the plot to reposition it.

Export

PNG (at the chosen size preset), clipboard copy, combined CSV (DSC interpolated onto the TGA grid), and settings JSON round-trip.
