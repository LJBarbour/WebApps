Plot Digitizer — guide

Extract numerical data coordinates from any graph image. The calibration uses independent projection for each axis, so it works correctly on rotated, zoomed-in, or non-standard-aspect figures.

Workflow

1.Drop (or browse) an image onto the canvas.
2.Enter two known X values (X₁, X₂) and two known Y values. Tick log scale if an axis is logarithmic.
3.Click Begin calibration then click the four reference positions on the image in order.
4.Click data points to record them. Use multiple series to digitize different data sets in one session.

Log-scale axes

Tick X log or Y log before calibrating. Enter the raw values (e.g. 0.01, 100) — the app converts to log space internally. The exported coordinates are always in original (linear) units.

Series

Click + Add series to create additional colour-coded datasets. Click a series chip to make it active. Each series is exported as its own CSV column (merged export) or individually.

Image tools

The Brightness and Contrast sliders adjust the displayed image to help with faint or low-contrast plots. They do not affect the saved CSV coordinates. Click Reset image to restore 100 / 100.
